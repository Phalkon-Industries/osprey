Contributors

Everyone who worked on the project belongs here. The first row is you; OSPREY pre-fills your name, ORCID iD, and primary institution when you're signed in with ORCID. For each collaborator, search ORCID by name to attach a verified iD. The display name and affiliation pre-fill from the ORCID record; you can edit either after.

Listing someone is quiet: they can view the project but aren't notified until you send a confirmation request or publish. The send buttons also save the draft. Project Editor access takes effect once they accept their credit.

Lineage

Link this project to any OSPREY projects it builds on. There are two kinds of link. Derived From means you started from their files or design and modified them (e.g. a fork, a rewrite, or a redesign). And Uses means their project is used inside yours, unmodified (like a module or a library component).

The linked project's team is notified and can dispute a link at any time if they feel it is invalid. Link claims are public and carry your name, so be sure they are accurate.
