OSPREY Contributor Guidelines

These are still being worked out. If something here doesn't fit how your group works, say so in the Suggestion Box.

Who to list

List everyone who did the work: design, build, firmware, testing, documentation. The contributor list is the credit record for the project, and it goes into the Zenodo record and the citation, so leaving someone out is a real omission.

If you are uploading a project you didn't build, list the real authors and give yourself a role like "Uploaded project". You stay the Project Owner on OSPREY either way.

ORCID iDs

Every contributor on a published project needs an ORCID iD. Attach it with the search on the submission form; it fills in the name and affiliation. A collaborator without an ORCID iD can get one for free at orcid.org in a couple of minutes.

Roles

Roles are free text. Keep them short and honest: "Project lead", "Firmware", "Mechanical design", "Field testing", "Uploaded project". The suggestions on the form are a starting point, not a list you have to pick from.

Accepting credit

Listing someone is quiet. They can see the draft, but nothing links to their profile until they accept the credit from Settings, under Contributor Credits. Send a confirmation request from the Contributors tab when the row is ready, or wait for publishing, which sends one to everyone listed.

Editing

The Project Owner controls the contributor list and is the only person who can publish. Ticking Project Editor on a row lets that person edit everything except the contributor list, once they have accepted their credit.

Removals and disputes

On a draft, a listed person can leave on their own. On a published project, credit is part of the record, so removal goes through staff: ask from Contributor Credits and staff will work it out with the project team.
