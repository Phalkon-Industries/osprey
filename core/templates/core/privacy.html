Privacy

OSPREY stores as little about you as it can get away with. This page says exactly what it keeps and why.

What OSPREY stores

  • Your ORCID iD and public profile basics (name, and whatever you add to your OSPREY profile: display name, bio, institution, avatar). Sign-in runs through ORCID; OSPREY never sees or stores a password, and it does not keep ORCID access tokens after sign-in completes.
  • An email address, only if you add one. It is used for exactly one thing: notifying you about activity on OSPREY — questions on your projects, replies to your threads, use reports, wiki suggestions. No marketing, no newsletters, and it is never shared with or sold to anyone.
  • What you publish here: projects, wiki edits, discussion posts, use reports. That content is the point of the platform and is public by design (drafts stay private).

Your controls

  • Every notification email carries one-click unsubscribe links, and notification settings has a master switch.
  • Removing your email address deletes it outright and cancels any queued mail. Nothing keeps a copy.
  • Email is kept until you remove it or your account is deleted, and no longer.

Questions or requests about your data: use the feedback widget or contact the maintainer.
